PAGE 02 — PRINCIPLES
01

NURTURING

I want the people around me to feel supported, seen, and capable of more than they thought. That shows up in how I give feedback, how I hold space for uncertainty, and how I celebrate the success of others.

02

LEANING IN WITH CURIOSITY

My instinct when something's unclear isn't to fill the gap with assumptions — it's to ask more questions. I find ambiguity genuinely interesting. It usually means there's something worth understanding underneath. I've found that curiosity is contagious in a room: when a researcher approaches a problem with open, genuine interest, the people around them tend to start doing the same.

03

BUILDING ALIGNMENT AND SHARED UNDERSTANDING

I'm often described as the person in the room who gets everyone speaking the same language. The most important thing I can do on any project is to make sure the people who need to act on it are already moving in the same direction before the findings happen. I design scoping, synthesis, and co-working workshops so teams reach insight and know how to act on that insight together.

04

TRANSPARENCY

I'd rather name what I don't know than project confidence I haven't earned. I share my thinking early and often, including the parts that are still rough. That kind of openness tends to make everyone around me more willing to participate throughout the research process, and avoid conflict when the results of that research are socialized.

05

MENTORING AND COACHING

I've mentored graduate students at the University of Michigan and served as a formal mentor at JSTOR, where my mentee was promoted from Associate User Researcher to User Researcher. Helping someone grow into their own voice is some of the most satisfying work I do. I deeply value education, and continually coach my organization on research best practices and how to listen to the audiences we serve.

06

AUTHORITATIVE, BUT NEVER AUTHORITARIAN

I take an evidence-informed approach to everything I do. I'm comfortable saying "I don't know — but we'll figure it out," and I mean it. I take deep pride in my craft and in the work I put into the world, but I don't need to announce it. Expertise shows in the work. Quiet confidence.
